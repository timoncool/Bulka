# A free AI agent: no key, no sign-up

The Bulka AI agent is **free** again. Requests go straight from your browser to [OVHcloud AI Endpoints](https://endpoints.ai.cloud.ovh.net/), an open free service with open-weight models (GPT-OSS, Llama, Qwen, Mistral) hosted in the EU.

## How to use it

1. Open the **Chat** tab in the side panel
2. In the settings, pick the **Free (OVHcloud)** provider
3. Pick a model and talk to the agent

No key, no sign-up, no subscription. It works out of the box.

## Good to know

- **Privacy.** The request goes from your device straight to OVHcloud: Bulka does not proxy it through its server. The data is not used to train models.
- **Limit.** The free tier allows 2 requests per minute (per IP). If you hit the limit, wait ~30 seconds.
- **Need more?** Add your own key (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini) or OpenRouter in the same settings for higher limits and quality.

Details on the [Free AI (OVHcloud)](/en/learn/free-ai) page.
